The Recognition Problem

This is a series of short stories set in the near future. Each is about AI and its increasing influence on society.

Every story is self contained and complete in itself.

However there is a thin thread connecting them to each other.

The 10 stories are meant to be read in sequence from beginning to end. They form the canon.

However, each chapter also has branches — marked by a small vertical bar in the right sidebar. This is an indication that at this point in the narrative there is a branch that offers you an alternative ending.

If you click to choose the branch, the story rewrites in real time from that point on. You can now read an entirely different version of events.

If you ever want to return to canon, just click on the button that says so at the top.

Oh and one other thing…

Every word of this story was written by AI.

Every. Single. Word.
