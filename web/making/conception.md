## Why a Forest Edition

*Short paragraph: the linear novel exists. Why the branches.*

The branches are not what-ifs. They are alternative readings of the same character at the same moment, given a slightly different decision. The reader who chooses one is not playing a game; they are receiving a different version of the chapter — one that obeys the same voice, holds the same architecture, and reaches a different end.
